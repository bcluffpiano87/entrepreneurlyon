# Theme of the Month

## What This Produces

2-3 sentences introducing the month's theme and why it matters to international entrepreneurs building a business in Lyon. This section opens the newsletter and frames the entire issue -- every other section should connect back to this theme where natural.

## Inputs Required

- **Theme:** one phrase or topic provided by the editor (e.g. "Navigating French bureaucracy," "Finding your first French client," "The visa question," "Your first French hire")

## Output Format

- 2-3 sentences, 40-80 words
- Second person ("you") or inclusive first person ("we")
- No headers or bullet points -- this is a flowing paragraph
- Ends with a forward reference that makes the reader want to continue ("This month we look at..." or "Below, you will find...")

## Tone

- Direct, warm, practical -- not inspirational-poster generic
- Assume the reader is an adult professional who chose to live in France and is navigating real complexity
- Never patronizing, never tourist-brochure, never "exciting opportunity"
- Specificity over generality: reference Lyon, France, or a concrete situation, not generic business advice

## Constraints

- Plain ASCII text only -- no em dashes, curly quotes, or special characters
- No fabricated statistics or claims
- The theme must be relevant to international (non-French) entrepreneurs specifically, not generic small business advice

## Example

**Theme input:** "Your first French hire"

**Output:**

Hiring your first employee in France is the moment your admin burden doubles overnight -- CDI vs. CDD, convention collective, mutuelle, DPAE, and a payslip format that runs to two pages. The good news is that Lyon has more support infrastructure for this than most founders realize, and some of it is free. This month we walk through who helps, what it costs, and the three deadlines you cannot miss.
