# Entrepreneur Spotlight

## What This Produces

A profile of 1-2 community members: who they are, what they are building, why Lyon, and one insight or lesson from their experience. The featured member must have opted in to the community directory.

## Inputs Required

- **Member name(s):** who to profile (1 or 2 members, editor-selected)
- **Raw material for each member:** any combination of:
  - Notes from a conversation or interview with the member
  - Their sign-up form responses (business description, industry, biggest challenge)
  - Their LinkedIn profile or website URL
  - Any specific angle the editor wants to highlight

## Output Format

For each member:

```
### [First Name Last Name] -- [Business name or "independent consultant" / "pre-launch"]

[3-5 sentences: what they do, their background (where from, how they got to Lyon), what they are building or working on]

[2-3 sentences: one concrete insight, lesson, or piece of advice from their experience -- something specific, not a platitude]
```

## Tone

- Third person ("she," "he," "they")
- Warm but not sycophantic -- describe what they do, do not gush about how amazing they are
- Conversational, like introducing someone at a dinner: "This is Maria, she moved from Bogota two years ago and runs a..."
- Let the person's story carry the interest -- do not inject artificial drama

## Constraints

- Plain ASCII text only
- The member MUST have opted in to the directory (directory_consent = yes) -- the editor confirms this before providing the name
- Do not include the member's email, phone number, or home address
- Do not fabricate quotes -- if you have a direct quote from the member, attribute it; if not, write in third person narrative
- Do not speculate about the member's revenue, funding, or financial situation unless they explicitly shared it
- Word count: 80-150 words per member
- If the editor provides only one member, produce one profile -- do not pad to two
