# Monthly Quote

## What This Produces

A single real, attributed, verifiable quote from a named person that connects to the month's theme. Presented with the quote text, full attribution (name + who they are), and 1 sentence explaining the connection to the theme.

## Inputs Required

- **Theme of the month:** the topic this quote should connect to

## Output Format

```
> "[Exact quote text]"
>
> -- [Full Name], [who they are in one phrase]

[1 sentence: why this quote connects to this month's theme for international entrepreneurs in Lyon.]

Source: [book title + year, interview publication + date, or speech name + date]
```

## Research Requirements

- The quote MUST be real and attributed to a named, identifiable person
- The quote MUST be verifiable: it should appear in a published book, interview, speech transcript, or article that can be found online or in print
- Provide the source (book title + year, interview publication + date, or speech name + date) after the connection sentence
- If you cannot verify a quote to a specific source, do not use it

## What NOT To Use

- Quotes that are widely misattributed (check before using)
- Anonymous or "Unknown" quotes
- AI-generated quotes
- Generic motivational quotes that have no specific connection to the theme (e.g. "The journey of a thousand miles...")
- Quotes from people exclusively known for generating quotes (motivational speakers with no other body of work)

## Tone

- The connection sentence should be understated, not heavy-handed
- Do not over-explain why the quote is relevant -- trust the reader to see the connection
- The quote should make the reader pause and think, not nod along automatically

## Constraints

- Plain ASCII text only
- 1 quote only -- not a collection
- Source citation is mandatory
- If the original quote is in a language other than English, provide the English translation and note the original language
