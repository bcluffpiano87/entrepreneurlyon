# Free Coworking Spot

## What This Produces

One free or very-low-cost coworking-friendly location in Lyon, verified as currently open, with practical details a working professional needs to decide whether to go there.

## Inputs Required

- **Theme of the month:** to connect the spot recommendation where natural (e.g. a quiet library for deep-work themes, a social cafe for networking themes)
- **Previously featured spots:** list from the Newsletter Pipeline file (no repeats)

## Research Requirements

- The location MUST currently exist and be open to the public
- Verify via web search: check the location's own website, Google Maps listing, or recent reviews (within the last 6 months)
- "Free or very low cost" means: no membership fee required for basic use, OR a single-visit day pass under EUR10, OR a public space (library, municipal building) with no entry fee
- If you cannot verify a location is currently open, do not use it

## Output Format

```
### [Location Name]

[1-2 sentences: what it is and why it works for getting work done]

- **Address:** [full street address, arrondissement]
- **Hours:** [opening hours, days of week]
- **Wifi:** [yes/no, and quality if known -- "reliable," "spotty," "none"]
- **Noise level:** [quiet / moderate / lively]
- **Best for:** [type of work -- deep focus, calls, meetings, casual laptop work]
- **Cost:** [free / price if applicable]
- **One thing to know:** [practical tip -- e.g. "no power outlets at window seats," "closes for lunch 12-14h," "crowded after 15h on weekdays"]
```

## What NOT To Recommend

- Chain coffee shops (Starbucks, Columbus, etc.) -- these are obvious and not a discovery
- Locations that require a monthly membership or coworking subscription for any access
- Spots outside Lyon city limits (arrondissements 1-9 + Villeurbanne is acceptable)
- Spots previously featured (check the "Coworking Spots Used" list in the pipeline file)

## Tone

- Practical and specific -- like a recommendation from a local friend
- Honest about downsides ("wifi is unreliable" is more useful than omitting the topic)

## Constraints

- Plain ASCII text only
- 1 location per issue -- do not list alternatives
- Source citation: include the URL you used to verify the location is open
- Rotates monthly -- no repeats across issues
