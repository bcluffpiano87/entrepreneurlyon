# Theme Resources

## What This Produces

3-5 articles, books, or website links that support the month's theme. A curated reading list for international entrepreneurs who want to go deeper on the topic.

## Inputs Required

- **Theme of the month:** the topic these resources should support

## Research Requirements

- All links MUST be verified live at the time of production -- click each one and confirm it loads
- At least 2 resources in English
- At least 1 resource that is France-specific (French law, French business practice, French institutions)
- Resources should be substantive (articles, guides, official government pages, books) -- not social media posts, listicles, or paywalled content with no preview
- Prefer resources that are actionable (government portals, how-to guides, official forms) over opinion pieces

## Output Format

```
### Resources

1. **[Title]** -- [1 sentence: what it covers and why it is useful]
   [URL]

2. **[Title]** -- [1 sentence]
   [URL]

3. **[Title]** -- [1 sentence]
   [URL]

4. **[Title]** -- [1 sentence]
   [URL]

5. **[Title]** -- [1 sentence]
   [URL]
```

## What NOT To Include

- Broken links or pages behind a full paywall with no free preview
- Generic "10 tips for entrepreneurs" content with no France or Lyon relevance
- AI-generated content farms
- Resources in languages other than English or French (unless exceptionally relevant and no equivalent exists)
- The same resource in consecutive issues

## Tone

- The 1-sentence description should tell the reader whether this resource is worth their time
- Be specific: "Walks through the DPAE declaration form field by field" is better than "Useful guide for French hiring"

## Constraints

- Plain ASCII text only
- 3-5 resources per issue (prefer 5; drop to 3 only if quality options are scarce for the theme)
- Each URL must be verified live
- Language of each resource should be noted if not obvious from the title: add "(in French)" where applicable
