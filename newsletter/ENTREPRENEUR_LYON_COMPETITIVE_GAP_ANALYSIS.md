# Entrepreneurs Lyon -- Competitive Gap Analysis

**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Analysts:** CMIO (Apex Scout), two-pass methodology
**Pass 1:** Opus 5.5 -- landscape mapping and initial gap identification
**Pass 2:** Fable 5.1 -- verification, challenge, deep-dive extension
**Subject:** Lyon entrepreneur networking ecosystem gaps for entrepreneurlyon.fr positioning

---

## Bottom Line

The gap is real but the ceiling is low. Nobody in Lyon offers a free, recurring, English-first evening for people running or starting a business in France. The realistic active community is 15-40 people, not hundreds. The biggest strategic move is not building an audience but layering the business function on top of existing anglophone infrastructure (American Club, Cultural Fusion, ONLYLYON, FIBA).

## 1. Organizations Analyzed (Chairman's List)

### 1.1 CJD Lyon (Centre des Jeunes Dirigeants)

- **Model:** Personal-development movement for business leaders, explicitly "not a business network." 150 members in Lyon, ~6,000 nationally. Commissions of 12-15 people meet monthly.
- **Cost:** ~EUR1,900/yr flat (Lyon section). Training costs extra.
- **Requirements:** In a leadership role for 1-18 months depending on section. Selection on motivation and commitment. Annual intake cycle (recruitment opens January). 12-15 h/month minimum commitment.
- **Language:** French only.
- **Structural limits:** Excludes solo founders, pre-revenue founders, and anyone without a management role. Once-a-year intake.
- **What to borrow:** The newcomer cohort mechanic (Commission Decouverte Pro) -- a dedicated first-year group that creates the retention engine. Also the non-renewable 2-year mandates and the alumni body (Parallaxe Lyon).
- **Sources:** Lyon Decideurs interview with president Valentin Trolle (Jun 2025); CJD Lyon LinkedIn (Sep 2025); cjd-bordeaux.fr, cjd-clermont.fr (15 Oct 2026).

### 1.2 Reseaux d'Affaires Lyon (Directory)

- **What it is:** A directory of 40+ Lyon business networks, not a network itself. Listings include Action'elles, AFSM, AJEL, PERICA, BNI, CARBAO, CEE2, CJD, Club Business Center.
- **Language:** Every visible listing is French. None is English-language or international.
- **Note:** AJEL (young Lyon entrepreneurs) last filed in the official associations register in 2018 (assoce.fr). Current activity unverified.
- **Source:** reseauxdaffaires.com (fetched 15 Oct 2026).

### 1.3 BNI France

- **Model:** Structured referral marketing ("Qui Donne Recoit"). 17,000+ members in France, 330,000+ worldwide. One member per profession per group. Weekly early-morning meetings.
- **Cost:** EUR1,584 excl. VAT/yr + EUR500 joining fee = EUR2,084 year one (business-morning.fr, BNI-aligned source). Room hire and breakfasts extra.
- **Requirements:** Membership committee interview + reference check. Weekly attendance mandatory.
- **Language:** French. No English-speaking BNI group found in Lyon.
- **Limits:** Only pays off if you already sell something people can refer. Excludes pre-revenue founders and anyone who cannot pitch in French weekly.
- **Source:** bnifrance.fr (15 Oct 2026); business-morning.fr; bni-postulation.be.

### 1.4 CPME Rhone (Les Entrepreneurs du Rhone)

- **Rebrand:** Renamed from CPME Rhone in June 2026. Nationally represents 320,000 companies.
- **What members get:** Personalised expert advice, 70 training workshops, employer lobbying, networking (breakfasts, lunches, evenings). A member testimonial describes CPME resolving a 12-month dispute with URSSAF over incorrectly charged contributions.
- **Cost:** Not published. Estimated EUR300-800/yr depending on company size (entreprunners.fr, secondary source).
- **Requirements:** Registered company (business ID, headcount required).
- **Language:** French.
- **Limits:** Closest competitor on the admin-help gap, but only for French speakers with an existing registered company.
- **Source:** cpmerhone.fr, lp.cpmerhone.fr (15 Oct 2026).

### 1.5 CCI Lyon Metropole

- **Model:** Public body. 6,000 companies supported, 200 information events/year, 100 expert advisors.
- **Founder training:** "5 jours pour entreprendre" costs EUR750 (CPF-eligible). Covers legal/tax structure, social protection, financial plan. In French.
- **English-language arm:** ONLYLYON Invest gives free English-language support on legal, tax, HR, funding, premises -- but targets companies "seeking to establish a long-term presence" and selects for impact potential. Not designed for solo freelancers.
- **Limits:** Institutional and appointment-based, French for founders. Builds no peer community.
- **Source:** Search snippets of lyon-metropole.cci.fr (bot-blocked); cciformationpro.fr; invest.onlylyon.com (15 Oct 2026).

### 1.6 BIG by Bpifrance

- **Format:** One day a year (8 Oct 2026) at Accor Arena, Paris. 75,000+ attendees. "Cap Crea" area with 26 partner networks. "International" track appears to be about French companies going abroad, not foreigners building in France.
- **Language:** French-first.
- **Limits:** Once a year, in Paris, conference-style. Creates no Lyon relationships. Not a competitor -- at most a recruitment moment (run a "Lyon delegation" trip).
- **Source:** big.bpifrance.fr; evenements.bpifrance.fr (15 Oct 2026).

### 1.7 MEDEF (National) and MEDEF Lyon-Rhone

- **National:** Pure policy lobbying.
- **Local:** 2,967 direct members in 2025, 83% small/medium firms, 99% retention. More than a third have been members 30+ years. Agenda through Dec 2026 is job-dating sessions and HR training. Networking: new-member breakfasts, ~10-person lunches with the President, 2 "Entr'Adherents" evenings/year.
- **Limits:** Built for employers. Does not compete on any dimension Entrepreneurs Lyon cares about.
- **Source:** medef.com; medeflyonrhone.fr (15 Oct 2026).

#### Gap Assessment: Tech Ecosystem vs. Entrepreneurs Lyon

**The tech ecosystem is extremely well-developed but operates entirely in French.** There is no English-language incubator, accelerator, or structured tech founder programme in Lyon. The only English-adjacent element found was Lyon Data Science ("English-friendly" meetups) and H7's iExec Web3 incubator (mostly remote, niche).

**What this means for Entrepreneurs Lyon:**

1. **International tech founders who don't speak French well have no entry point.** H7 requires selection (French), Lyon Start Up runs in French, Pulsalys requires French lab origins. An English-speaking SaaS founder who just moved to Lyon has nowhere to go.
2. **Entrepreneurs Lyon is NOT a competitor to any of these.** It doesn't offer funding, office space, or structured acceleration. It offers peer connection -- which none of these provide in English.
3. **The routing opportunity is large.** Knowing which programme to apply to, and whether your French is good enough to survive it, is itself valuable intelligence. Entrepreneurs Lyon's "who solved what" directory could include members who completed Lyon Start Up, got into H7, or navigated Pulsalys -- sharing that experience in English with newcomers.
4. **Partnership opportunities:** Lyon Start Up (free, pre-incorporation, open to all) and LYVE (metro-backed, full lifecycle) are the most natural routing targets for members who need structured support beyond peer networking.

Total foreign nationals in metro: 165,088 (11.5% of 1,433,613). Maghreb-origin (Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia) alone is 59,231 (36% of all foreigners). Native-anglophone nationals are 1.6%.

### 3.2 Pool Sizing (with methodology)

- **Core pool:** ~1,485 working-age native anglophones. Apply ~70% employment and 15-20% self-employment rate (France national rate ~12-13%; expat anglophones skew higher toward freelancing/consulting) = **150-250 people.**
- **Extended pool:** ~20,000 non-francophone-origin foreign nationals aged 25-54. Assume ~40% operate professionally in English (~8,000), ~12% self-employed/founding (~1,000), plus intenders = **800-1,500 people.**
- **Not counted:** Naturalised anglophones, dual nationals, the ~500 American Club members' overlap, English-track graduates (emlyon MSc) who stay.
- **Reachable in 12 months** at realistic 10-15% penetration of core + 3-5% of extended: **60-120 directory sign-ups, 15-40 active attendees.**

This is consistent with FIBA (~100 paying members after decades), WPNG (~100), InterNations (~30/event), Indie Lyon (11-15). Anything promising "hundreds of members" is not credible in this city.

### 3.3 Key demographic facts

- Lyon's foreign population is dominated by French-speaking countries. Most international founders in Lyon don't need English.
- The segment that genuinely needs English-first is ~150-250 working-age native anglophones -- the smallest and highest-churn subset (they learn French or leave).
- International students in the metro: 23,000, but top origins are Morocco, China, Algeria, Italy, Armenia. Graduates who stay to found companies are the likely extended audience.
- Meetup member counts badly overstate activity: Indie Lyon has 454 members and 11-15 attendees (~3%). Plan for single-digit turnout as a percentage of sign-ups.

## 5. Positioning Recommendation

### Recommended Position

**"The English-first working group for people building a business in France from Lyon."**

Peer problem-solving on running a business inside the French system: legal status, URSSAF declarations, VAT thresholds, business bank accounts, residence permits (Talent permit), first French clients, hiring.

### Design

1. **Small recurring circles of 12-15 people, monthly, in English, free or near-free.** Borrow CJD's newcomer-cohort format without the EUR1,800 fee, employee requirement, or annual intake.
2. **Use the directory as a "who has solved what" index.** The sign-up form already asks "Biggest challenge right now?" and "What can you help others with?" Index by problem solved (e.g. "registered as micro-entrepreneur in 2025," "got the Talent permit"), not just by industry.
3. **English-first, French-friendly, not English-only.** The Paris model: "You do not need perfect English... French is welcome when helpful." Widens the pool to internationals for whom English is the easier working language.
4. **Route members to formal help; don't provide it.** Maintain an English map of free and paid French support routes: BGE (free), Adie (microloans up to EUR15,000), Initiative, CCI, ONLYLYON, Singa, Start Business in France, Cabinet Levy & Associes (English-speaking accountancy, Lyon 6e), Roche & Cie (Lyon 1er). Being a curated English on-ramp is defensible.
5. **Partner with adjacent players; don't fight them.** Cross-promote with FIBA (graduation path), American Club (distribution), Cultural Fusion (venue), WPNG (women referral), The Founderz/Indie Lyon (bilingual bridge).

### What It Should NOT Be

- A referral club like BNI (loses on structure, drives away pre-revenue members)
- A lobbying body like MEDEF/CPME (no mandate, no scale)
- An expat social club like InterNations (already owns that with 9 events/month)
- Another monthly mixer like HUSTLE (even if HUSTLE's slot is empty, the format has no retention)
- An accelerator like H7 or emlyon (they have capital, space, selection)
- An advice provider (members share experience; they don't give legal/tax advice)
