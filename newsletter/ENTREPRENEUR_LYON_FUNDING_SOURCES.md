## Corrections to Common Assumptions (Read First)

1. **ACRE for micro-entrepreneurs was cut in half on 1 July 2026** -- exemption is now 25%, not 50%. (service-public.gouv.fr, decret of 6 Feb 2026)
2. **There is no "Initiative Lyon Metropole."** The Initiative France platform for Lyon is **RDI -- Rhone Developpement Initiative**. Beaujolais Initiatives covers Villefranche; Rhone Pluriel Initiative covers Vienne.
3. **NACRE no longer exists** (devolved to regions in 2017). Its AURA successor is the Region's "Commerce & Artisanat -- Creation/Reprise" scheme, delivered by BGE/CCI/CMA/Initiative/Adie.
4. **Adie's cap is EUR15,000, not EUR12,000** (and it is not interest-free -- only the EUR3,000 "Apport en capital" top-up is 0%).
5. **The EUR25,000 single VAT threshold was abandoned** (law of 3 Nov 2025). Micro thresholds remain EUR37,500 services / EUR85,000 goods for 2026.

## 1. Interest-Free Loans (Prets d'Honneur)

A pret d'honneur is a personal 0% loan to the founder (not the company), unsecured, to be injected as equity. Its real function is leverage: Initiative France reports banks lend EUR9.50 for every EUR1 of honour loan. Foreign nationals qualify if they hold a residence permit allowing self-employment. None of these networks has a nationality condition.

### 1.1 Pret d'honneur Initiative -- via RDI (Rhone Developpement Initiative)

- **French name:** Pret d'honneur creation-reprise
- **English:** Honour loan for business creation or takeover
- **What it is:** The Lyon-Metropole platform of Initiative France (1st French honour-loan network). Personal 0% loan + 3 years of post-creation follow-up + optional mentoring.
- **Who qualifies:** Anyone creating/taking over a business in Metropole de Lyon, Est Lyonnais or Monts et Coteaux du Lyonnais (58 communes incl. Lyon, Villeurbanne, Venissieux, Vaulx-en-Velin, Bron). Must work full-time in the business, with a financing plan > EUR15,000 and a bank loan in the plan. Excluded: real estate, export-only activity, night-time activities. No nationality condition.
- **How much:** EUR6,000 per full-time job created, cap EUR18,000 (3 FTE). Repayable over max 4 years. National network range is EUR3,000-EUR50,000, average EUR10,000.
- **How to apply:** Call RDI (mornings) 04 37 28 68 68 -> diagnostic interview -> build financing plan with a charge de mission -> pitch to a volunteer committee -> signature and disbursement. Allow 1-3 months.
- **Lyon contact:** RDI, Lyon -- rdi.asso.fr. Regional umbrella: Initiative Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes, accueil@initiative-auvergnerhonealpes.fr, 100+ reception points.
- **Sources:** aide-pour.fr fiche DGE/ISM n2237 validated 30/03/2026; mesaidespubliques.infogreffe.fr (updated 22/04/2026); initiative-france.fr; initiative-auvergnerhonealpes.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** RDI will lend you up to EUR18,000 at 0% interest, in your own name, with no collateral -- and French banks typically lend nearly ten times that amount on the back of it. You need a project worth more than EUR15,000 and a plan to work on it full time. Call 04 37 28 68 68 in the morning; the process takes one to three months.

### 1.2 Pret d'honneur Innovation "Start-up & Go" -- RDI

- **What it is:** Innovation variant of the RDI honour loan under the Region AURA "Start-up & Go" scheme.
- **Who qualifies:** Innovative creation/takeover projects (pre-creation or within 18 months of creation) in Metropole de Lyon, Monts et Coteaux, CCEL. Innovation must be certified by a regional committee. Bank co-financing required.
- **How much:** Up to EUR50,000 at 0%.
- **How to apply:** Contact RDI; pre-selection committee -> regional innovation characterisation -> bank discussions -> final committee.
- **Sources:** rdi.asso.fr/offres-de-services; dora.inclusion.gouv.fr (updated 15/09/2026).
- **Newsletter blurb:** If your project is genuinely innovative, RDI's "Start-up & Go" loan goes up to EUR50,000 at 0%. You can apply before you incorporate or up to 18 months after.

### 1.3 Reseau Entreprendre Rhone

- **French name:** Pret d'honneur Reseau Entreprendre
- **English:** Entrepreneurs' Network honour loan
- **What it is:** Peer-to-peer network of ~280 Lyon business owners who select "laureates" and mentor them monthly for 2-3 years, plus a personal 0% loan. 53 laureates in 2025, EUR1.79M in honour loans granted, 90% five-year survival rate.
- **Who qualifies:** Creation, takeover or growth projects with significant job creation (the network's standard criterion is ~5 jobs within 3 years; takeovers typically need a target with >= EUR500k turnover and 5 staff). Founder must manage the business as main activity and bring equity roughly matching the loan requested. No nationality condition stated.
- **How much:** EUR30,000-EUR60,000 at 0%, no guarantee. Bpifrance co-funds via a matching honour loan (EUR1 Bpifrance per EUR2 network loan).
- **How to apply:** reseau-entreprendre.org/rhone -> application -> 6-8 one-to-one validation interviews with business owners -> selection committee.
- **Sources:** reseau-entreprendre.org/rhone (Rapport d'activite 2025, published Oct 2026); LinkedIn RE Rhone 01/06/2026; subventions-facile.fr (updated 21/04/2026).
- **Newsletter blurb:** If you plan to hire -- think five jobs in three years -- Reseau Entreprendre Rhone lends laureates EUR30,000-EUR60,000 at 0% and pairs you with an experienced Lyon CEO for monthly mentoring over three years. Selection is tough, but it is the single most credible stamp you can get from the Lyon business establishment.

### 1.4 Adie (Association pour le Droit a l'Initiative Economique) -- Microcredit

- **Important:** Adie's main loan is NOT interest-free. Only the companion "Apport en capital" is 0%.
- **What it is:** Microcredit for people banks will not finance, plus free coaching before and after launch.
- **Who qualifies:** Any adult with a business project who is unlikely to get a bank loan -- employed, unemployed, student, on RSA, already a micro-entrepreneur, even banking-banned (interdit bancaire). Needs a personal guarantor for 50% of the loan (or a 25% deposit). Non-EU nationals need a permit allowing self-employment (Adie publishes a dedicated guide).
- **How much:** EUR300-EUR15,000, up to 48 months, 3-month deferral possible. Rate from 8.4% fixed (since 6 July 2026) + 6% solidarity contribution; worked example APR 16.54%. Plus "Apport en capital": up to EUR3,000 at 0%, deferrable up to 36 months. Decision in ~10 days, funds in 48 h.
- **How to apply:** Book online at agences.adie.org or 09 69 32 81 10. Bring ID, proof of income, 3 months of bank statements.
- **Lyon contact:** Adie Lyon 7 -- 2 avenue Leclerc, 69007; Adie Lyon 9 -- 49 avenue Rosa Parks, 69009 (inside Pole LYVE Lyon Ouest). Mon-Fri 9:00-18:00.
- **Sources:** adie.org/pour-creer-ou-developper-mon-entreprise; agences.adie.org (Lyon pages); dora.inclusion.beta.gouv.fr (updated 14/11/2025).
- **Newsletter blurb:** Banks said no? Adie says yes to almost anyone with a credible plan: up to EUR15,000 (at around 8-9% interest, so not free) plus a genuinely interest-free EUR3,000 top-up, answer in 10 days, money in 48 hours. You need one friend or relative willing to guarantee half the loan. Two Lyon offices, in the 7th and 9th.

### 1.5 Bpifrance Honour Loans (via the networks above)

- **Pret d'honneur Creation-Reprise Bpifrance:** EUR1,000-EUR80,000, 1-7 years, 0-24 months deferral; granted only through an accompanying network (Initiative/RDI, Reseau Entreprendre), at EUR1 Bpifrance per EUR2 network loan (creation) or 1:1 (takeover).
- **Pret d'honneur Solidaire:** EUR1,000-EUR8,000, 1-5 years, for young/women/unemployed/minimum-benefit recipients/QPV residents, with bank co-financing of at least equal amount.
- **Pret d'honneur Quartier (Entrepreneuriat Quartiers 2030):** EUR1,000-EUR15,000, 0%, for founders living or domiciled in a Quartier Prioritaire (QPV) or within 300 m. Must be a French tax resident and hold shares. Up to 66% of total need. Lyon QPV areas: La Duchere, Mermoz, Venissieux Minguettes, Vaulx-en-Velin.
- **Sources:** bpifrance-creation.fr "Pret d'honneur Quartier"; Bpifrance Creation webinar deck "La garantie bancaire" (2025).
- **Newsletter blurb:** You never apply to Bpifrance directly for these -- RDI or Reseau Entreprendre do it for you, and Bpifrance adds its own 0% loan on top of theirs. If you live in a priority neighbourhood (check your address on the QPV map), a separate EUR15,000 0% loan exists.

## 2. Government Grants, Subsidies and Public Support

### 2.1 ACRE -- Aide a la Creation ou a la Reprise d'une Entreprise

- **English:** Start-up social-charge relief
- **What it is:** Temporary reduction of social contributions in year one. Not automatic -- you must apply.
- **Who qualifies (micro-entrepreneurs):** One of: receiving ARE/ASP unemployment benefit; registered jobseeker unpaid > 6 months in last 18; on RSA/ASS; aged 18-25 (29 if disabled); under 30 without ARE rights; creating in a QPV or ZFRR zone; CAPE contract; PreParE recipient. Not benefited from ACRE in last 3 years. Nationality irrelevant.
- **How much:** Micro-entrepreneurs created from 1 July 2026: 25% reduction (rates fall to 9.3% goods / 15.9% BIC services / 19.2% BNC) until the end of the 3rd calendar quarter after start. Start your activity on 1 Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct to get the full 12 months.
- **How to apply:** Within 60 days of declared start date, on autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr (or URSSAF for companies), with the Guichet unique registration proof.
- **Sources:** service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F11677; entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/actualites/A18795; bpifrance-creation.fr; autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** ACRE knocks 25% off your social charges for your first year -- but only if you ask within 60 days of starting, and only if you fit a category (unemployed, under 26, living in a priority neighbourhood, etc.). Start your business on the first day of a quarter to squeeze the full 12 months out of it.

### 2.2 ARCE -- Aide a la Reprise ou a la Creation d'Entreprise

- **English:** Lump-sum conversion of unemployment benefits into start-up capital
- **What it is:** France Travail pays 60% of your remaining ARE unemployment rights as capital (minus 3% pension deduction), in two instalments: half at start, half six months later.
- **Who qualifies:** Registered jobseeker receiving ARE who creates/takes over a business in France after the end of their employment contract and who has obtained ACRE (mandatory). Second instalment requires the business still exists and you hold no full-time CDI. EU and non-EU nationals with French unemployment rights qualify equally.
- **Alternative:** Keep monthly ARE and combine with business income (ARE reduced by 70% of income) up to the same 60% ceiling. You must choose one.
- **How to apply:** Declare the project to your France Travail adviser, file the ARCE form, provide INPI synthesis/Kbis.
- **Sources:** service-public.gouv.fr F15252; francetravail.fr "aides 2026"; unedic.org.
- **Newsletter blurb:** On French unemployment and want to start a business? France Travail will hand you 60% of what you're still owed as a lump sum -- half now, half in six months. The catch: you must first secure ACRE, and you give up the monthly payments. Run the simulator on francetravail.fr before choosing.

### 2.3 Region Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes -- "Commerce & Artisanat: Creation/Reprise"

- **What it is:** FSE-co-funded regional scheme, operational for 2026 (1 Jan-31 Dec), delivered by labelled operators. The NACRE replacement.
- **Strands:**
  - **Structurer son projet** -- 100% paid-for individual coaching (~10 h over ~6 months) to build the business plan, choose legal form, find funding. Operators: BGE AuRA (services/other), CCI (industry/services), CMA (crafts).
  - **Financer ma TPE** -- for financing plans EUR20,000-EUR150,000: honour loans via Initiative (RDI) + 3-year follow-up.
  - **Volet micro-entreprise** -- for plans < EUR20,000 by people far from bank credit: Adie microcredit (EUR10-12k) + honour loan up to EUR5,000.
  - **Volet entreprise a potentiel** -- plans > EUR70,000 with > 5 jobs at 3 years: EUR30,000 honour loan, up to EUR60,000 if innovative (Start-up & Go), via Reseau Entreprendre/Initiative.
- **Who qualifies:** Anyone creating/taking over in AURA, "notably jobseekers". No nationality condition.
- **How to apply:** Direct to the operator (BGE: contact@bge-aura.fr; Initiative AURA; Adie; Reseau Entreprendre). Region contact: 101 cours Charlemagne, 69269 Lyon Cedex 02.
- **Sources:** auvergnerhonealpes.fr/aides (both fiches); bpifrance-creation.fr "dispositifs regionaux (ancien Nacre)" (Sept 2026); bge-aura.fr/formations/pcre; mesaidespubliques.infogreffe.fr (updated 04/05/2026); les-aides.fr (28/04/2026).
- **Newsletter blurb:** The Region will pay 100% of a professional coach to help you write your business plan and find money -- you pay nothing. Go to BGE for most service businesses, the CCI for industry, the CMA for crafts. Once your plan is ready, the same scheme routes you to a 0% honour loan sized to your project.

### 2.4 Cheques CREA (Region AURA)

- **What it is:** Vouchers paid by the Region to labelled operators for specific accompaniment services: project preparation (EUR630), test d'activite inside a cooperative d'activite (EUR315-EUR735), entrepreneur-salarie-associe (EUR700), honour loan/guarantee accompaniment (EUR1,050 each), microcredit (EUR945), post-creation follow-up (EUR420/EUR315/EUR210 over 3 years). One voucher per type per year.
- **Who qualifies:** Jobseekers, under-30s with student-entrepreneur status, or people in a demission-reconversion pathway.
- **Source:** fonds-publics.fr (scheme start 07/10/2025); bpifrance-creation.fr regional page. Caveat: no first-hand Region page with these amounts was retrievable; verify with BGE/CCI before publishing figures.
- **Newsletter blurb:** If you're unemployed or under 30, the Region funds your coach, your "test your business before you register it" period, and your post-launch follow-up through vouchers you never see -- the operator bills the Region. Ask BGE or your CCI adviser about "Cheques CREA".

### 2.5 Bpifrance -- Bourse French Tech

- **English:** French Tech Grant
- **What it is:** Non-repayable Bpifrance subsidy for innovation projects with high technological risk needing maturation/techno-economic validation. Open year-round, instructed by Bpifrance regional directorates.
- **Who qualifies:** EU-definition SME; or a natural person (incl. sole trader) if accompanied by a recognised incubator/accelerator with a selection committee. Company under one year old. No nationality condition.
- **How much:** Up to EUR30,000, covering 50-70% of eligible R&D spend. Bourse French Tech Emergence (deeptech): up to EUR90,000. No spend before filing counts. Paid in two tranches.
- **How to apply:** Online via Bpifrance; via your incubator (Lyon: H7, Pulsalys, Lyon Start Up alumni routes).
- **Sources:** fundguide.fr (status verified 02/09/2026 against bpifrance.fr); digifec.com; lafrenchtech-aixmarseille.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Bpifrance will give -- not lend -- up to EUR30,000 (EUR90,000 for deep-tech) to validate a risky technical idea. It's open all year. Solo founders can apply if an incubator vouches for them. Don't spend a cent on the project before you file.

### 2.6 Bpifrance -- Pret d'Amorcage (InvestEU)

- **What it is:** Unsecured loan to strengthen cash before a fundraise.
- **Who qualifies:** Innovative companies < 5 years that have already received a public innovation grant.
- **How much:** EUR50,000-EUR300,000, 8 years, 36-month deferral, no personal guarantee; 5% retention.
- **Source:** fundguide.fr (open, 02/09/2026); aides-territoires.beta.gouv.fr (20/02/2026).
- **Newsletter blurb:** Already won a Bourse French Tech? Bpifrance will then lend EUR50k-EUR300k over eight years with three years before you repay a cent, and no personal guarantee.

### 2.7 Bpifrance -- Garantie Creation

- **What it is:** Bpifrance guarantees your bank loan so the bank says yes and asks for less personal collateral.
- **How much:** 60% of the loan for ex-nihilo creation, 50% otherwise; up to 70% under the delegated "contrat de garantie" for loans <= EUR200,000. Personal guarantees capped at 50%, primary residence protected.
- **How to apply:** Your bank requests it -- you never apply directly. Ask explicitly.
- **Sources:** bpifrance.fr/catalogue-offres/garantie-creation-standard; economie.gouv.fr; Bpifrance Creation webinar deck.
- **Newsletter blurb:** When a bank hesitates, ask them to "mobiliser la Garantie Creation Bpifrance" -- the state covers 60% of their risk. Most bankers know it; not all offer it unprompted.

### 2.8 France Active Auvergne-Rhone-Alpes -- Bank-Loan Guarantees

- **What it is:** Guarantees for founders far from bank credit; avoids personal cautions in 90% of cases.
- **Products:**
  - Garantie Egalite Femmes (women, jobseekers/precarious): 80% cover, <= EUR50,000, <= 84 months, 2.5% one-off fee, no personal caution.
  - Garantie Emploi (jobseekers/precarious workers): 65%, <= EUR50,000.
  - Garantie Egalite Acces (long-term unemployed, RSA, under-26 precarious, disabled): 80%, <= EUR50,000.
  - Garantie Egalite Territoires (QPV/ZFRR): up to 80%, <= EUR100,000.
  - Garantie Impact (ESS/impact companies): 65%, <= EUR100,000.
- **Lyon contact:** France Active AURA, 2 place Andre Latarjet, 69008 Lyon, 04 78 75 72 34 -- franceactive-aura.org.
- **Sources:** bpifrance-creation.fr (two guarantee pages); franceactive-aura.org; economie.gouv.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Women founders, jobseekers and anyone living in a priority neighbourhood can get France Active to guarantee up to 80% of a bank loan of EUR50,000 (EUR100,000 in QPV areas) for a one-off 2.5% fee -- and the bank cannot then ask for your house as security. Office in Lyon 8th.

### 2.9 Metropole de Lyon -- LYVE

- **What it is:** The Metropole's free entrepreneurship platform: 300 experts, six programmes, four Poles d'Entrepreneurs.
- **Programmes:** EARLYVE (free, open to all, idea stage workshops); Lyon Start Up (see 2.10); LYVE IN (4-month commercial-strategy incubation, 12 places/cohort, EUR150 HT/month); LYVE UP (up to 3 years of individual coaching + subsidised space, companies < 4 years); Grand 8 (growth); Pepites (scale-up SMEs).
- **Money:** LYVE itself gives no grants, but the Metropole voted EUR587,928 in 2026 operating subsidies to the accompaniment ecosystem.
- **Contact:** lyve-lyon@grandlyon.com
- **Sources:** grandlyon.com LYVE page; lyve-lyon.com; Metropole press release 16/02/2026.
- **Newsletter blurb:** LYVE is the Metropole's free front door for entrepreneurs: workshops, 300 experts, a community platform and four physical hubs. Start with EARLYVE (free, no selection) at 49 avenue Rosa Parks in Lyon 9 -- Adie is in the same building.

### 2.10 Lyon Start Up

- **What it is:** Free 4-month programme (120 h of training) for innovative project holders who have not yet incorporated, twice a year, 100 projects per cohort, 95% go on to create. Pitch contest with cash prizes.
- **How much:** 1st EUR10,000, 2nd EUR5,000, 3rd EUR3,000, coups de coeur EUR500.
- **How to apply:** lyonstartup.com. Contact: lyonstartup@lafrenchtech-stl.com, 2 avenue Tony Garnier, 69007 Lyon.
- **Sources:** lafrenchtech-stl.com; lyonstartup.com (12/12/2025, 19/02/2026); LinkedIn 23/06/2026.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Got an idea but no company yet? Lyon Start Up gives 100 founders a semester of free training and a shot at EUR10,000. Programme is in French; apply before you register the company.

### 2.11 Region AURA -- R&D Booster

- **What it is:** Collaborative R&D call (company + regional research lab), TRL 5-8, labelled by a pole de competitivite.
- **How much:** Up to 60% of eligible costs for SMEs; project budget EUR250k-EUR1M, eligible base >= EUR200k; 24 months max.
- **Status:** 2026 call closed 3 March 2026; expect a late-2026 opening for 2027.
- **Sources:** cara.eu; lyonbiopole.com (cahier des charges 2026); fundguide.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** If you can partner with a Lyon university lab on a EUR250k+ R&D project, the Region covers up to 60%. The 2026 round is closed -- watch for the 2027 call around November.

### 2.12 Entrepreneuriat Quartiers 2030 / French Tech Tremplin

- **French Tech Tremplin (Incubation strand, 2026 edition):** for start-ups < 3 years old whose founder (>= 25% equity) is a QPV/ZRR resident, OFPRA-recognised refugee, under subsidiary/temporary protection, scholarship holder or minimum-benefit recipient. Max EUR15,000 direct funding + up to EUR12,000 of incubation paid, 12 months.
- **CitesLab Lyon** (Maison Lyon Emploi): free, confidential coaching for QPV residents from idea stage, funded by Bpifrance/Region/Metropole/Ville.
- **Sources:** bpifrance.fr reglement 2026 (PDF); entrepreneuriat-quartiers-2030.fr; maison-lyon-emploi.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Refugee status or a priority-neighbourhood address unlocks a dedicated channel: French Tech Tremplin (EUR15,000 + a paid incubator seat) and CitesLab's free one-to-one coaching in Lyon.

## 3. Tax Credits

### 3.1 CIR -- Credit d'Impot Recherche (Research Tax Credit)

- **Rate (2026):** 30% of eligible R&D spend up to EUR100M, 5% above. Refundable in cash for SMEs/JEI (not just offset).
- **Who:** Any company under real tax regime doing R&D (resolving scientific/technical uncertainty). Micro-entrepreneurs cannot claim (not au reel).
- **Source:** impots.gouv.fr; fundguide.fr status 02/09/2026.
- **Newsletter blurb:** France refunds 30% of genuine R&D costs in cash, even if you're loss-making. If you employ a developer or scientist solving hard problems, get a CIR specialist to look -- but note it does not cover routine development.

### 3.2 CII -- Credit d'Impot Innovation

- **Rate:** 20% of prototype/pilot-installation costs for new products, capped at EUR400,000 spend/year -> max EUR80,000 credit. Extended to 31 Dec 2027. SMEs only.
- **Sources:** entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr F35494; impots.gouv.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Building a prototype of a product that doesn't exist yet on the market? The CII refunds 20% of that cost, up to EUR80,000 a year. Runs until end-2027.

### 3.3 JEI / JEC / JEII -- Jeune Entreprise Innovante

- **What:** Status for companies < 11 years (created before 31 Dec 2028), SME, >= 20% of expenses in R&D (JEC: 5-20% with growth criteria; JEII "JEI a impact" created by the 2026 Finance Act), 50% capital held by individuals/VCs/etc.
- **Benefit:** Exemption from employer social contributions on R&D staff -- up to EUR8,401.58 salary/month per employee, cap EUR240,300 per establishment per year. Income-tax exemption abolished for companies created from 1 Jan 2024.
- **Sources:** entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr F31188; impots.gouv.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Spend a fifth of your costs on R&D and JEI status wipes out most employer social charges on your researchers and developers -- worth roughly 30-40% of their salary. No longer gives a corporate-tax holiday for companies created after 2023.

### 3.4 IP Box (article 238 CGI)

- **What:** Optional regime taxing net income from patents, utility certificates, and original copyright-protected software at 10% instead of 25% corporate tax.
- **Sources:** bofip.impots.gouv.fr BOI-BIC-BASE-110-10/-20; inpi.fr; Deloitte Avocats blog (2026).
- **Newsletter blurb:** If your revenue comes from licensing software or patents you developed, you can elect to pay 10% tax instead of 25% on that slice. Software must be "original" and you must track R&D costs per product -- set up the accounting from day one.

### 3.5 Abolished / Expired (Do Not Publish as Live)

- Credit d'impot formation des dirigeants -- ended for training after 31 Dec 2024.
- Aide France Num EUR500 cheque -- closed.
- ADEME Tremplin transition ecologique -- closed.
- JEI income-tax exemption -- not for companies created >= 2024.

### 3.6 Related Employer Aid

- **Apprenticeship hiring aid** (contracts signed from 8 March 2026, starting before 1 Jan 2027), employers < 250 staff: EUR5,000 (aide unique, CAP-Bac levels), EUR4,500 (Bac+2), EUR2,000 (Bac+3 to Master); EUR6,000 for a disabled apprentice. First year only.

## 4. Micro-Entrepreneur Specific

- **2026 contribution rates (URSSAF):** 12.3% goods / 21.2% BIC services / 25.6% BNC (non-regulated liberal) / 23.2% Cipav regulated professions / 6% classified tourist rentals. Versement liberatoire adds 1% / 1.7% / 2.2%.
- **2026 regime ceilings:** EUR203,100 goods, EUR83,600 services (prorated in year one).
- **VAT franchise 2026:** EUR37,500 services (tolerance EUR41,250), EUR85,000 goods (tolerance EUR93,500). The EUR25,000 single threshold was abandoned.
- **ACRE:** 25% reduction (see 2.1) -- start on a quarter boundary, apply within 60 days.
- **Adie:** the de-facto lender for micro-entrepreneurs (Section 1.4).
- **Region AURA micro strand:** microcredit EUR10-12k + honour loan <= EUR5k for plans < EUR20k (Section 2.3).
- **CFE:** exempt in creation year but only if you mail form 1447-C-SD to your SIE by 31 December of that year; 50% base reduction year two; permanent exemption if turnover <= EUR5,000.
- **Dedicated bank account:** mandatory only once turnover exceeds EUR10,000 two years running (micro); mandatory from day one for companies with share capital.
- **Sources:** entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr F36232, F21746, F23999, F23257, F37369; urssaf.fr; economie.gouv.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** Three micro-entrepreneur money facts for 2026: (1) your social charges are 12.3%, 21.2% or 25.6% of revenue depending on activity -- ACRE cuts that by a quarter in year one if you apply within 60 days; (2) you charge no VAT until EUR37,500 (services) or EUR85,000 (goods) -- the rumoured EUR25,000 threshold is dead; (3) you owe zero CFE business tax in your first year, but only if you post form 1447-C to the tax office before 31 December.

## 5. International / Foreign Entrepreneurs

### 5.1 Residence Status (The Gate)

- **EU/EEA/Swiss:** no permit needed.
- **Non-EU -- permits that allow self-employment:** carte de resident (incl. longue duree-UE, Algerian 10-year), vie privee et familiale, entrepreneur/profession liberale (1 year, renewable; must show viability and SMIC-level income), Talent -- porteur de projet (up to 4 years), carte "recherche d'emploi ou creation d'entreprise" (post-graduation, 1 year), temporary-protection card (Ukrainians).
- **Permits that do NOT allow it:** salarie, travailleur temporaire, etudiant (students must switch status), visiteur.
- **Sources:** entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr F22494; adie.org fiche "nationalite etrangere"; shine.fr blog.
- **Newsletter blurb:** No French funding body asks about your passport -- but every one of them asks about your residence permit. If yours says "salarie", "etudiant" or "visiteur", you cannot legally run a business; apply to the Prefecture for an "entrepreneur/profession liberale" card first.

### 5.2 Carte "Talent -- Createur d'entreprise"

- **Thresholds (2026):** Master's degree or 5 years' comparable experience; a "real and serious" project certified by the Ministry of Economy (attestation valid 12 months); investment >= EUR30,000 in the project (own or borrowed funds); personal resources >= annual gross SMIC = EUR22,404.20. Up to 4 years, renewable, family included.
- **Sister routes:** "Talent -- projet economique innovant" (French Tech Visa for Founders -- no investment threshold, needs recognition by an incubator/DRIEETS; resources >= SMIC); "Talent -- investisseur" (EUR300,000 in tangible/intangible assets); "Talent -- mandataire social" (salary >= 3x SMIC = EUR67,212.60).
- **Sources:** service-public.gouv.fr F16922; welcome.businessfrance.fr; demarches.numerique.gouv.fr; entreprises.gouv.fr.
- **Newsletter blurb:** The 4-year "Talent -- Createur d'entreprise" card needs a Master's (or 5 years' experience), EUR30,000 invested in your business -- loans count -- and proof you can live on at least EUR22,404 a year. Get the ministry's project certificate first; it's valid 12 months.

### 5.3 SINGA Lyon

- **What:** Free 9-month incubator (March-November) for newcomers to France (refugees or any other residence permit) or locals with a migration-related project. Individual follow-up, workshops, mentors, desk at Le Faitout tiers-lieu. 700+ people from 83 nationalities since 2018; 61% women.
- **Requirements:** B2 French or English (workshops in French; A2 French advised); 2 days/week in Lyon (Thu/Fri).
- **Calendar:** Expect 14th-cohort call ~December 2026. Contact: isciane@singa.fr / 06 95 53 55 85.
- **Sources:** singalyon.fr (23/12/2025); entrepreneuriat-quartiers-2030.fr; LinkedIn SINGA 29/01/2026.
- **Newsletter blurb:** SINGA Lyon is the one Lyon incubator explicitly built for people who just arrived in France -- and it accepts B2 English. Nine months, free, with a desk. Applications open each December for a March start.

### 5.4 What Does NOT Exist

- Any grant or loan restricted to, or enhanced for, foreign nationals as such. French public funding is residence-permit-gated, not nationality-targeted.
- Any Franco-British or Franco-American bilateral funding programme operating in Lyon. The chambers (FIBA, EACC) are networking bodies, not funding sources.
- A live Region AURA innovation grant for solo start-ups outside R&D Booster (collaborative) and Start-up & Go (honour loan).

## 7. Banking and Practical Finance

### 7.1 The Nuclear Option: Droit au Compte

If any bank refuses your company an account, demand a written refusal letter, take it to the Banque de France, and it will designate a bank legally obliged to open a basic business account.

### 7.2 Online Banks for Foreign Entrepreneurs

- **Qonto:** accepts all French legal forms incl. micro-entrepreneurs and EI; can open the account and receive capital deposit before registration (France/Germany only); RCS-registered foreign companies accepted if parent is in DE/ES/IT/UK.
- **Shine (Ageras):** accepts residence-permit holders but refuses "salarie", "etudiant", "travailleur temporaire", "visiteur" permits; non-French tax residents accepted only for existing companies, not for capital deposit/creation.
- **Revolut Business:** French-registered companies, director may live abroad, local French IBAN, account in ~24 h; does not accept sole traders/micro-entrepreneurs, charities, cooperatives.

### 7.3 Common Pitfalls

- **URSSAF:** declare monthly or quarterly even at zero revenue ("neant"); missing declaration = EUR60.10 penalty per declaration + 5% (monthly) / 15% (quarterly) surcharge.
- **CFE:** form 1447-C-SD by 31 Dec of creation year or you lose the first-year exemption; notice is online-only, never posted.
- **TVA:** two different ceilings (micro regime vs VAT franchise). Breaching the upper tolerance means VAT from that very day.
- **ACRE 60-day window** and ARCE's ACRE prerequisite -- missing the first kills the second.

- **Newsletter blurb:** Rule one: if a bank refuses you, get it in writing and go to the Banque de France -- they will force a bank to open your account. Rule two: online banks are faster (Qonto even accepts your capital deposit before the company exists) but check your residence permit type -- Shine rejects student and employee permits outright. Rule three: the three fines that catch newcomers are the missed URSSAF "zero" declaration (EUR60 each time), the unposted CFE form (costs you a year's exemption) and the VAT tolerance ceiling (VAT due from the day you cross it).
