Entrepreneur Lyon

Welcome. We are glad you found us.

Building a business is never easy, and finding the right people, those who understand what you are going through and can genuinely help, can feel even harder. Entrepreneur Lyon exists to change that. We are a community of international entrepreneurs based in Lyon who meet regularly to share what we know, learn from each other, and lend a hand whenever we can.

Joining takes a minute. You decide what you want us to know about you: the types of events you enjoy attending, days and times you are most often available, the hurdles you are facing, and what topics you are most interested in. This helps us plan meetups and events on days and at times that are most convenient for our members. It also helps us seek out the kind of guest experts to invite to our events that serve our community best and that our members can truly benefit from.

Want the community to know more about what your business or your skillset offer? Tell us more: what your business specializes in, what services or skills you offer, and how you can help others in the community. Everyone is invited to have their services displayed on the Member Services page. Don't want your information to be displayed? Not a problem! You can choose to keep your information private.

You've found us, now come be part of the community.
