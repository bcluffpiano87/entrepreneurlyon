Entrepreneur Lyon

Welcome. We are glad you found us.

Building a business is never easy, and finding the right people, those who understand what you are going through and can genuinely help, can feel even harder. Entrepreneur Lyon exists to change that. We are a community of international entrepreneurs based in Lyon who meet regularly to share what we know, learn from each other, and lend a hand whenever we can.

Joining takes a minute. Share just your name, email, and availability, or tell us more: what your business does, and how you can help someone else in the group. Either way, you will be part of something built for exactly this.
